How an engagement works.

Every project follows the same path. It keeps scope honest and makes sure what we build gets used.

  1. Start with the decision

    We agree on the questions that matter and who needs the answers before touching any data.

  2. Fix the foundation

    We connect, clean and model the data so the numbers hold up under scrutiny.

  3. Build it with you

    We deliver in working pieces, with your team reviewing as we go.

  4. Hand over and improve

    You get documentation and training, and we stay close as needs change.
