Market overview

Where the major indices stand, and what changed.

The S&P 500 lost 0.5% over the past week and is up 11.4% year to date. It sits 4.6% above its 200-day average, a long-term uptrend by that measure.

Over the past month the S&P 500 led the major US indices at -1.0%, while the Nasdaq 100 trailed at -5.0%.

By sector, energy (+10.8%) and utilities (+8.1%) led over one month, while communication services (-7.4%) lagged.

The US 10-year yield is 4.99%, down 17 basis points in a month, and the VIX is at 19.5.

What the legends are doing

Holdings from each investor’s latest SEC 13F filing, largest portfolio first, updated automatically within a day of a new filing. Select an investor for their top holdings and recent buys and sales.

13F filings arrive up to 45 days after each quarter ends and cover US-listed stock positions only: no short positions, cash, bonds or most foreign shares, so a fund’s real size and cash are larger than shown. Filings do not report what an investor paid, so purchase prices are estimates unless marked as reported. Not a recommendation.

Research notes

The analysis behind the numbers.

Method

The same rules every day, applied to every company.

Closing prices only

Returns use adjusted daily closes, so dividends and splits are accounted for. Nothing on this page is estimated or back-filled.

Relative to peers

Valuations are compared with the median of the same industry, and analyst targets are weighted toward the most recent revisions.

Measured in public

Every signal is recorded forward from the day it appears, never back-filled, and compared with the S&P 500 over the same days, whether it worked or not.
