Market overview

Where the major indices stand, and what changed.

The S&P 500 gained 1.2% over the past week and is up 13.1% year to date. It sits 7.5% above its 200-day average, a long-term uptrend by that measure.

Over the past month the Nasdaq 100 led the major US indices at +4.7%, while the Russell 2000 trailed at -5.6%.

By sector, technology (+7.5%) and communication services (+0.6%) led over one month, while utilities (-8.5%) lagged.

The US 10-year yield is 5.18%, up 52 basis points in a month, and the VIX is at 14.9.

Research notes

The analysis behind the numbers.

Method

The same rules every day, applied to every company.

Closing prices only

Returns use adjusted daily closes, so dividends and splits are accounted for. Nothing on this page is estimated or back-filled.

Relative to peers

Valuations are compared with the median of the same industry, and analyst targets are weighted toward the most recent revisions.

Measured in public

Every flag and position is recorded with its date and compared with the S&P 500, whether it worked or not.
